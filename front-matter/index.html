Front Matter

How This Book Works

The front matter is part of the product, not just packaging. It tells instructors how to adopt the book, tells learners how to move through it, makes the five-lens design visible before Chapter 1, and defines the project spine that runs across the whole book.

FM

Front Matter Package

A publisher-facing and reader-facing opening package that clarifies audience, pedagogy, assets, and chapter conventions.

Preface How to Use Instructor Guide Companion Assets
Included Components
  1. Preface and market positioning: why synthetic-data-first multimodal AI deserves a dedicated book.
  2. How to use this book: course path, practitioner path, research path, philosopher path, teacher path, and accelerated path.
  3. Case-study guide: the three recurring systems and how they reappear across chapters.
  4. Visual pedagogy plan: architecture diagrams, data schemas, labeling examples, evaluation dashboards, and deployment flowcharts.
  5. Companion package: notebooks, starter repos, datasets, slides, rubrics, project templates, and audio companion material.

FM2

How to Read, Study, Teach, and Think With This Book

Defines the internal chapter template and shows how the same material should serve builders, learners, researchers, deep thinkers, and instructors.

Detailed Sections
  1. The standard chapter pattern: motivation, intuition, formal core, practical tools, evaluation, recap, exercises, and project milestone.
  2. How a self-learner should move through microlearning blocks and checkpoints.
  3. How an instructor should map chapters to lectures, labs, and assessments.
  4. How a researcher should distinguish established knowledge, best practice, emerging direction, and unresolved question.
  5. How a philosopher-reader should look for assumptions, tradeoffs, and conceptual tensions.
