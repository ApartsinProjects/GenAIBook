Front Matter

Publisher-Ready Opening Package

This front matter is intentionally structured as the book's editorial launchpad: first the promise, then the audience, then the reading logic, then the course pathways, then the recurring case-study spine, and finally the companion assets and instructor kit. Throughout, the book is positioned around one continuous workflow: synthetic data is designed, generated, filtered, and then used to train, fine-tune, benchmark, stress-test, and refresh models.

Editorial Intent This section should read like the opening of a serious education and engineering title: it establishes the market promise, the teachability of the manuscript, the case-study spine, and the adoption logic before the reader commits to the main technical arc.
