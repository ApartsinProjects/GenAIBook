Building Vision and Audio AI with Deep Generative Models

This book teaches readers how to design synthetic-data-first AI systems for image, audio, video, and multimodal applications, then turn those systems into reproducible experiments, deployable products, and credible research contributions. This edition is explicitly redesigned across five lenses: practitioner, student, researcher, philosopher, and teacher.

Core promise: build, evaluate, teach, and intellectually understand a synthetic-data-driven multimodal system from idea to capstone or paper.

Five Lenses

  • Practitioner:
    tools, workflows, debugging, deployment, and modern stacks.
  • Student:
    scaffolded sequencing, manageable units, and checkpoints.
  • Researcher:
    methodology, benchmarks, ablations, and open problems.
  • Philosopher:
    mental models, assumptions, tradeoffs, and “why”.
  • Teacher:
    lectures, labs, assessments, and semester-long project flow.

Audience

  • Primary:
    Graduate students and advanced practitioners in ML, computer vision, speech, robotics, and applied AI.
  • Secondary:
    Advanced undergraduates and engineers transitioning from model usage to system design.
  • Not for:
    Complete beginners without willingness to use the appendices.

Teaching Spine

  • Recurring case studies:
    Industrial inspection, multilingual speech/audio analytics, and multimodal incident understanding.
  • Every chapter includes:
    Learning outcomes, system milestone, common pitfalls, and suggested exercises.
  • Tracks:
    Hands-On systems work and Research extension prompts run in parallel.
