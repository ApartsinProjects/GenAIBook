Application Blueprint: Industrial Inspection and Manufacturing

Shows how synthetic image and video data support rare-defect coverage, inspection reliability, and deployment on production lines.

Core Undergraduate Graduate Engineering Research Companion Repo
Blueprint Template
  • Problem profile -> Data strategy -> Model and system stack -> Evaluation and deployment -> Governance and research angle.
Detailed Sections
  1. Problem profile: defect taxonomies, edge cases, station layout, review latency, and cost of false negatives versus false positives.
  2. Data strategy: procedural rendering, simulator-driven scenes, controlled editing, and hybrid real-plus-synthetic sampling for scarce defect classes.
  3. Model and system stack: segmentation, detection, anomaly scoring, retrieval-assisted review, and operator-facing inspection interfaces.
  4. Evaluation and deployment: slice design across lighting, angle, material, speed, and rarity, plus line-side deployment and human-review station patterns.
  5. Governance and research angle: traceability of synthetic defects, acceptance thresholds, and research questions around rare-event generalization.
