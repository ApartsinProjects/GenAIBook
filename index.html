Building Vision and Audio AI with Deep Generative Models

Subtitle: Synthetic Data, Multimodal Systems, and Real-World Fine-Tuning. This book is designed to take advanced students, instructors, and working engineers from problem framing through synthetic data design, model selection, fine-tuning, evaluation, deployment, and research-quality reporting across image, audio, video, and multimodal systems.

Book promise: readers leave with a deployable multimodal prototype, a reproducible evaluation package, and a clear path to a capstone, workshop paper, or teachable course module.
